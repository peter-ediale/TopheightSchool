We are proud to share the results of our **2026 West African Senior School Certificate Examination (WASSCE)** class.

All **22 candidates** from Topheight College earned a credit or better in **both English Language and General Mathematics**, the two subjects every university and employer looks at first. Every candidate also secured at least five credits overall.

Across the class, students earned **more than 50 A1 distinctions** in subjects including Physics, Chemistry, Economics, Agriculture, Geography, Christian Religious Studies and Civic Education.

## Behind the results

These results were not won in the exam hall alone. They come from years of steady teaching, practical lessons in our science laboratory, and students who chose to work hard. Our candidates also wrote their papers here at Topheight, in our own approved WAEC centre, in classrooms they know well.

Congratulations to the class of 2026, and thank you to our teachers and parents.
