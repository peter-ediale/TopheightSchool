We are delighted to welcome you to the new website of **Topheight Schools**, home of Topheight Academy (Nursery & Primary) and Topheight College (Secondary) in Akwofor, Asaba.

Since 2015, our school has grown from a small beginning into a community of pupils, teachers and families who share our motto: **Humility, Empathy and Prospect**.

## What you will find here

- Information about [Topheight Academy](/academy) and [Topheight College](/college)
- How to [apply for admission](/admissions) and book a school visit
- Details for schools wishing to register candidates at our [WAEC examination centre](/waec-centre)
- Photos from the laboratory, Career Day and everyday school life in our [gallery](/gallery)

We will share news, events and achievements here regularly. If you have a question, [contact us](/contact) or send us a message on WhatsApp.
