## Step 1: Google Search Console (15 minutes, most important)
This tells Google the website exists and lets you see what people search to find you.

1. Go to **search.google.com/search-console** and sign in with the school's Google account.
2. Click **Add property** → choose **URL prefix** → type `https://topheightschool.pages.dev/` → Continue.
3. Under *Other verification methods*, choose **HTML tag**. You'll see something like:
   `<meta name="google-site-verification" content="AbC123xyz..." />`
4. 📩 **Send Claude the code inside `content="..."`**. Claude adds it to the website and pushes; it goes live in about 2 minutes.
5. Back in Search Console, click **Verify**.
6. In the left menu open **Sitemaps** → type `sitemap-index.xml` → **Submit**.
7. Open **URL inspection** (top search bar), paste `https://topheightschool.pages.dev/` → **Request indexing**. Do the same for `https://topheightschool.pages.dev/news/wassce-2026-results/`.

Google usually shows the site in results within a few days to two weeks.

## Step 2: Google Business Profile (the Maps listing, 20 minutes)
Parents searching "schools near me" or "schools in Asaba" mostly see the **Maps listing**, not websites. Your listing already exists as **"Topheight Academy"**.

1. Go to **business.google.com** and sign in. If the profile isn't there, search "Topheight Academy Asaba" on Google Maps and tap **"Own this business?" / "Claim this business"**, then follow Google's verification (often a short video of the gate, signboard and classrooms).
2. **Website:** add `https://topheightschool.pages.dev`
3. **Phone:** add 0803 889 4126 as the second number.
4. **Categories:** main category **Private school**; additional: **Nursery school**, **Primary school**, **Secondary school**.
5. **Description:** paste the *long description* above.
6. **Photos:** upload the **logo**, a **cover photo** (the College building), and 10+ good photos: the lab, Career Day, assembly, the compound. Add new photos every month; active profiles rank higher.
7. **Post an update:** create a post about the WASSCE 2026 results with a link to `https://topheightschool.pages.dev/news/wassce-2026-results/`.

⚠️ **About the name.** Google requires the profile name to match the name on your **signboard**. The signs currently say "Topheight College", the profile says "Topheight Academy". **Don't** rename it to "Topheight Schools" until there's a signboard or gate sign saying "Topheight Schools", or Google may suspend the listing. A "Topheight Schools" sign at the gate (showing Academy and College under it) would fix this properly; then rename the profile.

## Step 3: Google reviews (ongoing, the biggest ranking factor on Maps)
1. In your Business Profile, click **Ask for reviews** → copy the link.
2. Send it to happy parents and alumni on WhatsApp. Example message:

> Good day! Thank you for trusting Topheight with your child. We'd be grateful if you could take one minute to share your experience on Google. It really helps other parents find us: [paste review link]. Thank you! 🙏 Topheight Schools

3. Aim for **10 reviews in the first month**, then a steady trickle.
4. **Reply to every review** (thank them by first name). Never offer gifts for reviews; Google removes them.
5. 📩 Send Claude 2–3 of the best review quotes (with permission) to add to the website's testimonials section.

## Step 4: Bing and Apple Maps (10 minutes)
- **Bing Webmaster Tools** (bing.com/webmasters): sign in → **Import from Google Search Console**. This covers Bing, Yahoo, DuckDuckGo and several AI search tools.
- **Apple Business Connect** (businessconnect.apple.com): claim the school so it shows correctly in iPhone Maps. Use the same details as above.
